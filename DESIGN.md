## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, premium circular economy and environmental intelligence platform. It merges the austere precision of modern financial tools with an organic, clean sustainability ethos. The interface conveys institutional credibility, technological clarity, and environmental stewardship without resorting to rustic tropes.

Drawing from modern minimal fintech and refined tactile UI movements, the experience prioritizes light, luminous surfaces, generous negative space, crisp micro-interactions, and high-definition details. The emotional baseline is confident, transparent, and restorative: complex environmental lifecycle metrics feel actionable, orderly, and effortless.

## Colors

The palette leverages a strictly metered distribution to maintain high-end clarity:

- **Canvas & Primary Surfaces (75%):** Warm, pristine off-whites (`#FAFAF5`, `#F5F7F0`, and pure `#FFFFFF`) establish an airy, luminous ground that softens screen glare compared to harsh pure grays.
- **Support & Grouping Surfaces (20%):** Soft sage and desaturated mint tones (`#E8F3E8`, `#DDEBDD`, `#C7E8CA`) provide subtle segmentation for metric panels, active selections, and interactive wells without introducing heavy visual noise.
- **Accents & Telemetry (5%):** Pure emerald (`#16A34A`) is reserved strictly for primary interactive triggers, positive lifecycle states, and verified indicators. Deep forest greens (`#174B35`, `#103B2A`) supply deep grounding accents and contrast anchors.
- **Typography & Structure:** Primary text uses an ink-weighted deep evergreen charcoal (`#1C2B22`), muted functional copy uses balanced moss gray (`#4B6354`), and containment lines utilize whisper-thin borders (`#E2EBE1`).

## Typography

The typography pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and display layers with **Inter** for running copy, tabular layouts, and data points. 

Plus Jakarta Sans imparts geometric warmth, humanized curves, and a crisp contemporary presence to section headers and metric anchors. Inter delivers maximum neutral legibility across dense data tables, verification logs, and telemetry charts. 

Numerical figures and currency-equivalent impact metrics should employ tabular figures (`tnum`) to maintain structural vertical alignment across dashboard views.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an 8pt structural rhythm within a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (breakpoint: 1200px+), collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet (768px - 1199px), and a 4-column layout on mobile (<768px).

Margins provide ample breathing room around analytical modules to reflect the open simplicity of high-grade fintech software. Structural hierarchy is established primarily via horizontal cadence and nested container padding rather than dense graphical divider lines. Data cards and control modules snap strictly to layout bounds, maintaining consistent inner padding across similar structural tiers.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through translucent layers, subtle specular borders, and soft, plant-tinted ambient illumination:

- **Flat/Subsurface:** `#F5F7F0` or `#FAFAF5` canvas surfaces with a single `#E2EBE1` border line for clean separation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Panels):** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) backgrounds floating on `#FAFAF5`, bounded by a hairline border (`1px solid #E2EBE1`) and an ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Tactile Hover & Action Flyouts):** Lifted surfaces receiving direct interaction: `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(23, 75, 53, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Elevated dialogs supported by soft frosted backing panels (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px); background: rgba(250, 250, 245, 0.82)`) with a deep ambient envelope: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(16, 59, 42, 0.12)`.
- **Tactile Edge Highlights:** Interactive controls and primary cards leverage an ultra-subtle inset top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) to mimic precision-machined, clean physical hardware.

## Shapes

The roundedness tier `2` defines an organic yet strictly controlled geometry:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px) radius delivers structured discipline and tactile responsiveness.
- **Card Containers & Modules:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) radius creates a friendly, polished perimeter that balances geometric efficiency with natural curvature.
- **Large Panels & Dialog Surfaces:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) radius softens major viewport containers.
- **Circular Indicators & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill or 9999px geometry for continuous circular flow metrics and status badges.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid emerald (`#16A34A`) fill, pure white text (`#FFFFFF`), inset top highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`), and a subtle extruded drop shadow (`0 2px 4px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.2), 0 1px 2px rgba(16, 59, 42, 0.15)`). Hover increases brightness slightly and elevates shadow. Active state depresses 1px with inner shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#1C2B22` text, bordered by `1px solid #E2EBE1`. Hover transitions surface to `#F5F7F0` with border shifting to `#C7E8CA`.
- **Destructive / Alert:** Deep subdued crimson text with soft rose-tinted neutral surface; avoids competing with core emerald hierarchy.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2EBE1`, border-radius `0.5rem`. Placeholder text in `#4B6354` at 50% opacity. Focused inputs highlight with a clean `#16A34A` perimeter and a luminous halo ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Smooth squared-rounded and circular housings with `#FFFFFF` idle bases and `#E2EBE1` borders. Checked state triggers `#16A34A` background with crisp white iconography and scale transition.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Low-saturation tinted fills (`#E8F3E8`), deep forest labels (`#174B35`), and an optional 6px glowing `#16A34A` dot. Border set to `1px solid rgba(22, 163, 74, 0.15)`.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** `#FFFFFF` base, transitioning on selection to `#DDEBDD` with `#103B2A` text and `#16A34A` active perimeter.

### Cards & Metrics
- **Metric Tiles:** Crisp white `#FFFFFF` surface, `1rem` radius, subtle top specular edge, hairline `#E2EBE1` boundary. Micro-charts and sparklines use `#16A34A` paired with soft emerald gradient fills fade-dropping to transparent.
- **Lifecycle Tracking Nodes:** Stepped circular connector lines running horizontally; completed segments render in high-clarity `#16A34A`, pending paths in dashed `#DDEBDD`.

### Lists & Tables
- **Data Rows:** Delimited by clean `#E2EBE1` horizontal hair lines. Alternate row striping avoided; rows use a seamless hover shift to `#F5F7F0`. Cell text aligns strictly to numeric grid logic.
